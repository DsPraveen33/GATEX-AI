# Third-Party Notices and Architecture References

This project, **GATEX AI (Personal AI Study Agent for GATE 2027 CSE)**, incorporates structural concepts, educational patterns, and architectural references from the following open-source initiatives and community resources:

## 1. Reference Repositories & Architecture Inspirations
- **demovirat4-max/gate-cse-hub**: GATE subject organization, PYQ categorization, and AI mentor paradigms.
- **demovirat4-max/personal-gate**: Personalized preparation workflows and dynamic study dashboards.
- **shrikrishna97/gateprep-nexus**: MCQ/MSQ/NAT question formats, virtual calculator design, and offline persistence.
- **khushit-shah/gater-frontend**: PYQ browsing taxonomy, subject filters, and saved questions architecture.
- **eeshan15/GitGrind**: Spaced repetition models, preparation pulse, and data validation.
- **Inevitable12345/gate-coursework-multi-agent**: Multi-agent orchestration and specialized subject routing.
- **gate-exam/awesome-gate-cse-roadmap**: Curated GATE CSE subject roadmaps and topic sequencing.
- **GATEOverflow / GO-PDFs**: Historical GATE question archives and syllabus provenance.
